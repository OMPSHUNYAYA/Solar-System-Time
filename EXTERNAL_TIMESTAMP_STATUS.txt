SOLAR SYSTEM TIME — EXTERNAL TIMESTAMP STATUS v1.0.4

Historical project prediction/freeze hashes are recorded in the project ledger.
They were not, at the time of the historical blind test, anchored to an
independently controlled public timestamp included in this package.

Therefore:
- the hashes are useful integrity/commitment identifiers;
- this package does not claim they independently prove pre-truth publication;
- no retroactive timestamp can repair that historical limitation.

For a future new blind test, freeze the prediction artifact and anchor its SHA-256
through a public, independently timestamped mechanism before truth reveal (for
example a public repository commit, DOI-bearing archival deposit, or comparable
external timestamping service).
