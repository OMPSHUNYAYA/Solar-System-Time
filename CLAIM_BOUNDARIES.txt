SOLAR SYSTEM TIME — CLAIM BOUNDARIES v1.0.4

Permitted framing
-----------------
- bounded epoch recovery from declared planetary astrometric inputs;
- compact, dependency-free reference implementation;
- project-recorded blind-reserve results from two observational archives;
- reproducible synthetic solver self-test;
- standalone low-precision visual clock/demo.

Required qualifications
-----------------------
- celestial-configuration dating is an established idea, not claimed here as a
  new discovery about time;
- project-recorded historical result, not external endorsement/certification;
- independent third-party reproduction remains open;
- raw reserve observations are not included, so headline blind metrics are not
  reproducible from this package alone;
- historical project hashes are integrity/commitment records, not independently
  timestamped proof of chronology;
- catalogue timestamps were used upstream for packetization and chronological
  development/reserve partitioning;
- inverse resolver did not receive absolute observation epochs or relative
  within-packet time offsets;
- reference model is not ephemeris-free and is low precision relative to modern
  integrated ephemerides;
- active approximate-element validity window is 1800-2050 unless explicitly
  overridden for exploratory use.

Not claimed
-----------
- novelty of celestial dating / epoch recovery itself;
- replacement of UTC, TAI, GPS/GNSS, atomic clocks, or civil timekeeping;
- navigation-grade, sub-second, unrestricted all-time, or arbitrary-epoch timing;
- cryptographic proof that an event physically occurred at a claimed epoch;
- completed independent third-party reproduction;
- peer review, institutional certification, or external endorsement;
- astronomical validation from green package-check badges.
